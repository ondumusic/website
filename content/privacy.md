> **In short:** Ondu doesn't collect any data about you. The apps talk only to your own music
> server. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

This policy covers the Ondu apps for iPhone and Mac and this website. Ondu is made by
{{< publisher >}} ("I", "me").

## What the apps collect

Nothing. The apps contain no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and
no third-party code that sends data anywhere. I have no way to see who uses Ondu or how.

## What's stored on your device

- Your server address and username, and your sign-in details, which are kept in the system
  Keychain.
- A copy of your library: artist, album, song and playlist information, plus artwork.
- Any music you download for offline listening.
- Your settings and playback queue.

This stays in the app's private storage on your device. Signing out removes your library and
downloads, and deleting the app removes everything.

## What's sent to your server

The apps send the following information to your server, and only to that server:

- Your username and password when you sign in.
- Requests for your library, artwork and music.
- Play counts, so the server knows what you've listened to. You can turn this off in Settings.
- Changes you make to playlists.

The server is run by you. How that server stores and handles data is up to the
person running it, not me.

## Local network access

The apps may ask for permission to use your local network. That's only so they can reach a
server on your home network. They don't scan for or connect to other devices.

## This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages,
and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

Ondu doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.
